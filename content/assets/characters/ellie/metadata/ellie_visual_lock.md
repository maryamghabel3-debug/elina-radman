## مشخصات بصری تأییدشده توسط مالک (Owner-Confirmed Visual Specification)

### Character type
A minimalist, friendly, hand-drawn **white doodle** character. A simple rounded
humanoid drawing — **not** a realistic human child.

### Head
- Very large, horizontally oval head.
- In the frontal reference, approximately **twice the body's width**.
- Rounded, pure white interior.

### Face
- Two small **solid black circular dot eyes**.
- One tiny **curved smiling mouth** in the neutral reference.
- **No** nose, eyebrows, ears, hair, eyelashes, teeth, or detailed anatomy.

### Body
- Small, compact, rounded white torso.
- Short, chunky arms and legs.
- Rounded **mitten-like hands** with only subtle finger separation.
- Large, simple rounded feet.
- **No** built-in clothing or accessories.

### Linework
- Thin-to-medium **rough black pencil/charcoal outlines**.
- Slightly imperfect, hand-drawn contours.
- Multiple loosely overlapping strokes around the silhouette.
- **Preserve** the visible sketch lines.
- **Not** clean vector geometry or digitally perfect smooth contours.

### Fill
- Pure **white** face and body interiors.
- **No** internal shading, gradients, skin texture, fur, or colored fill.
- The pencil texture belongs to the **outlines**, not to a realistic body surface.

### Neutral reference presentation
- Full body, frontal standing pose.
- Warm off-white background.
- Generous negative space.
- Vertical **9:16** composition.

> **Important.** The neutral reference presentation is **not** a restriction on story
> shots. Camera angle, pose, framing, setting, and action **can change**. The tiny smile
> describes the **neutral reference expression** only — it does **not** become a
> requirement that Ellie must smile in every emotional scene. New expressions must
> retain the minimal design and be reviewed.

**Do not apply these corrections to Elina.** They concern Ellie's body design only.

### House story — «Elli بزرگ» و «Elli کوچک»

«Elli بزرگ» (big Elli) and «Elli کوچک» (little Elli) in the house story are
**narrative versions of Ellie** — narrative scale/role variants within the story, not
separate characters. **Do not silently replace the larger Ellie with Elina.**

### Separating identity from episode art direction

Separate **character identity** from **episode art direction**:
- A cinematic, realistic environment may surround the flat-drawn Ellie, but that
  environment is **not** part of Ellie's body design.
- **Do not** impose a universal ban on environmental shadows or mirror reflections —
  later story rooms require them. Ellie's body can remain flat white while the scene
  uses intentional shadows or reflections.
- Likewise, "no built-in accessories" does **not** prohibit story props, such as
  holding the pocket watch after the Waiting room.
