## 3. Koochooloo Concept Sheet (Top 3 Styles)

### Common Definition (All Styles)
- **Name (temporary):** Koochooloo (کوچولو)
- **Role:** Hidden inner child — vulnerable, unseen part of the psyche
- **Nature:** Non-human metaphorical being (never realistic child)
- **Size:** 25–30% of seated Elina’s height
- **Signature Gesture:** Gently pulling or holding Elina’s sleeve
- **Core Emotion:** Needs to be seen, without being demanding or cute
- **Relationship:** Elina does not fix, reject, or dramatically comfort. She simply acknowledges presence.

**Prohibited:** Realistic child face, plush toy, commercial doll, Pixar/Ghibli look, horror, over-cute, kawaii, extra limbs.

---

### Style A: Silhouette Paper-Cut Cinema

**Body & Form:**  
Petite, elegant silhouette. Slightly elongated limbs. Simple recognizable shape with long delicate arms. Head proportion slightly larger than body. Form reads clearly even in shadow.

**Eyes:** Deep cut-out shadow eyes (no pupils). Emotion conveyed through head tilt and posture.

**Texture & Palette:**  
Aged black paper with subtle grain. Slightly irregular handmade edges. Bone-white highlights. Very limited antique gold on cuff or heart area.

**Signature Behaviors (recurring):**
1. Softly pulling sleeve with low pressure
2. Head bowed, body partially hidden behind Elina’s arm
3. Reaching upward with both arms when seeking acknowledgment

**Entry/Exit in Reel:**  
Enters from lower frame edge as if cut paper is revealed. Exits by sliding into Elina’s shadow or folding away.

**Emotional Core:** “I have always been here.”

---

### Style B: Needle-Felted Textile Sculpture

**Body & Form:**  
Rounded, soft, slightly asymmetric. Large head, compact body. Visible wool fibers and needle marks.

**Eyes:** Simple hand-embroidered dark stitches (large, gentle).

**Texture & Palette:**  
Dense wool felt. Visible fiber texture. Cream-dawn gray-charcoal base with muted terracotta accent on small garment.

**Signature Behaviors:**
1. Clinging lightly to sleeve with felt hands
2. Leaning head against Elina’s arm
3. Small fingers tracing fabric texture of Elina’s clothing

**Entry/Exit:**  
Rises gently from lower frame like a soft textile being lifted. Dissolves slowly into the folds of Elina’s clothing.

**Emotional Core:** “I am soft. I am here. Please notice.”

---

### Style C: Ink-Wash Calligraphic Poetic

**Body & Form:**  
Fluid, elegant lines created from ink washes. Breathing negative space. Form appears to be in motion even when still.

**Eyes:** Deep concentrated ink dots with soft wash halos.

**Texture & Palette:**  
Aged paper grain + controlled ink bleeding. Charcoal to bone white with limited warm sepia/terracotta accents.

**Signature Behaviors:**
1. Ink lines gently extending to touch sleeve
2. Form slightly leaning or dissolving toward Elina
3. Head tilt that creates beautiful negative space around the figure

**Entry/Exit:**  
Appears as ink bleeding into existence from the corner. Fades by controlled bleeding back into paper texture.

**Emotional Core:** “I exist in the space between us.”

## 5. Visual Test & Consistency Plan

**Phase 1 — Reference Sheets (per style)**
- 12–15 angles + expressions
- Signature gesture variations
- Multiple lighting conditions

**Phase 2 — Fixed Scene Tests**
- Generate all 9 prompts above using identical Elina reference image
- Use character reference strength 0.75–0.85
- Record seeds and exact parameters

**Phase 3 — Consistency Tests**
- Generate 5 variations of the same prompt (same seed + reference)
- Test across 3 different aspect ratios
- Test low-light and high-contrast versions

**Phase 4 — Reel Motion Tests**
- Image-to-video tests (first 3 seconds)
- Focus on sleeve-pulling micro-movement
- Test entry/exit methods described in concept sheets

**Naming Convention**
`Koochooloo_[Style]_[Scene]_[Variation]_[Date].png`

**Golden Set Goal**
After testing, select 1–2 “Golden” images per style to use as permanent character references.
