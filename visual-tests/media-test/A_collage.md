A cream deckled sheet holds a composed painted society portrait, shifted to the lower right, with one corner of the picture peeling up like thin painted paper. Under the lift, only abstract childlike marks in faded ultramarine, muted plum, and vermilion appear—no child, no face. A single charcoal line runs the right margin; the upper-left field is empty paper.

Source artwork: Madame X (Virginie Amélie Avegno Gautreau), John Singer Sargent, 1883–84. The Metropolitan Museum of Art, object 12127.
